# Project Brief: First-to-Market Research Report

## What this project is
A narrative-driven, data-backed article for Rainfall Health's reports pipeline
(the same pipeline as california-rainfall.netlify.app), answering the research
question: **does it matter whether a company is first, second, or third to
market, however "first" is defined?**

This started as a companion exploration to Kyle's research into why Los
Angeles has only one hospital in Medicare's TEAM model, and expanded into a
standalone piece after Kyle wanted the general economics question tackled
on its own, modeled explicitly on that TEAM/Cedars-Sinai article's structure
and voice.

## Reference exemplar
The TEAM/Cedars-Sinai article ("Why Does Los Angeles Have Only One Hospital
in Medicare's TEAM Model?") is the style and structure target. It is included
in this project's source material (see uploaded file: index-narration.txt,
or ask Kyle for the original). Read it before writing or editing anything
here — the style guide in 01-style-guide.md is a distillation of it, but the
actual article is the ground truth.

## Audience and purpose, in Kyle's own words
> "imagine big systems, big industries... these are professionals, these are
> operation experts, these are people who know details... these are people
> who don't want to waste time... they can handle things that are super
> complicated, but they would rather have it simple and sophisticated and
> straight to the point. Not pushing an agenda, objective, just saying the
> facts... not as boring as an encyclopedia, but almost like reading the
> facts on a Snapple cap... so simple, easy delivery, so straightforward
> that you read it and you're like wait is that true."

This is written for Rainfall Health colleagues (SVP Revenue has already
seen and reacted well to the TEAM piece), not for a general public audience.
It may also be adapted for Artometrics later, but that is a secondary use,
not the primary target.
